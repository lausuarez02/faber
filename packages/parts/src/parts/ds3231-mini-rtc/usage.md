+ → 3V3, − → GND, D → the board's SDA (XIAO D4, Uno A4), C → its SCL (XIAO D5, Uno A5); NC stays free. The same DS3231 chip and code as the ZS-042 (RTClib: RTC_DS3231 rtc; rtc.begin(); rtc.now(); rtc.adjust(DateTime(...)) when rtc.lostPower()), on a 14 x 14 mm board with no power LED and no charging circuit: 0.11 mA, the low-power DS3231 for pocket devices. For a thin case cut off the 5-pin socket and solder wires (about 6 mm tall then; measure yours). Lowest current of all: rv3028-rtc (45 nA). In a device that deep-sleeps you can also power + from a GPIO and switch it off while asleep: the chip keeps time on its coin cell. A scenario's clock and set_clock set the time.
