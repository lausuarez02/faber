Battery devices: this ZS-042 board (38 x 22 x 14 mm with its CR2032) has a power LED that draws about 2 mA all the time, 15 times the clock: desolder the LED (or its 1 kΩ resistor) and set prop powerLed false, and remove the coin-cell charging diode (or fit an LIR2032), because the board charges the cell from VCC and a CR2032 is not rechargeable (worse at 5 V). For thin or low-power devices pick ds3231-mini-rtc (same chip and code, 14 x 14 mm, no LED, 0.11 mA) or rv3028-rtc (19 x 19 x 4 mm, 45 nA, plain Wire).
